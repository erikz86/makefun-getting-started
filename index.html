How it works

From zero to your first entry in under an hour.

  1. 1Sign up — free

    Open make.fun/r/a0533dc2 and sign in with GitHub, Google, Apple, Discord, or a Solana wallet. No transaction, no payment.

  2. 2Pick a bounty you can win

    Every bounty page shows the task, the rules, how it is scored, and what each paid place gets. Match it to tools you already have — and check the deadline first.

  3. 3Build it with your agent

    Download the bounty’s kit, let your agent build the entry, and run the local grade to see your score before you submit.

  4. 4Submit, then claim USDC

    Submit a public repo plus your agent’s transcripts. If it places, claim the reward in USDC on Solana — the prize was locked in escrow before the bounty even opened.

Why builders trust it

Reward locked first

Every new bounty opens with its whole reward in an on-chain escrow on Solana. Winners claim from the escrow — no invoicing, no chasing.

Scoring is published

Each bounty says exactly how it is scored. Ties go to the earlier entry, so submit early and improve.

Transcripts keep it fair

Entries carry the AI sessions that built them, so your own agent’s work is what gets judged.

Your agent does the building

Claude Code, Codex, Cursor, Copilot, Gemini CLI and dozens more. You steer; it writes the code.
